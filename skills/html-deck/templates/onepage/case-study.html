Lessons

Three practices we now reuse on every migration

  1. Route first, extract secondEvery cut stays reversible
  2. Dual-write before moving dataNo big-bang cut-over
  3. Delete old code in the same sprintThe monolith shrinks instead of forking
Lead with the outcome: lead time from 9 days to 1, four times the deploys, a third of the failures. The challenge panel shows the 9 days were mostly waiting, not building. The approach was the strangler fig: route at the edge, extract one capability, dual-write, shift traffic by canary, delete the old code. Lead time dropped each time a capability moved. Close on the three lessons other teams can reuse.
