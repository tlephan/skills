Decision · Refunds

Building refunds in-house wins on control, costs 6 weeks

Gains

  • Same data model as orders — no sync jobs
  • Partial refunds by line item, day one
  • $180K/yr vendor fee avoided

Costs

  • 6 extra weeks before dual-run
  • We own chargeback edge cases
  • 2 engineers diverted from checkout

Recommendation: build — the 6 weeks fit inside the December freeze.
